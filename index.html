Our story & atmosphere

Recipes from home in the DRC, served like family.

Deluxe Manna started with the dishes we grew up on: slow-grilled meats, cassava leaves simmered for hours, bread pounded from cassava. We cook every plate fresh to order, with the same patience and generosity as Sunday lunch back home.

The room pairs a modern lounge with warm African décor, and the music is Congolese, from classic rumba to today's ndombolo. Come for dinner and you might stay for the cocktails.
